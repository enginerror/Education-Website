Frequently Asked Question

What types of courses do you offer?

We offer a wide range of skill-based courses including technology, business, design, finance, and more.

Are the courses free or paid?

We offer both free and premium courses. You can start with free content and upgrade anytime.

Do I get a certificate after completing a course?

Yes, upon successful completion of a course, you'll receive a shareable certificate.

Can I access the courses on mobile?

Yes, our platform is fully responsive and works on all devices including smartphones and tablets.

Are there any live classes or only recorded videos?

We provide recorded video lessons. Some premium courses may include live sessions.

How can I track my learning progress?

You can track your progress through your dashboard, which shows completed lessons and course status.
